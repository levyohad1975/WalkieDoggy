1. **Owner:** authorize the capture session (`CAPTURE_SESSION_AUTHORIZATION.md` §11, decisions D1–D4) and arrange a second iPhone. Then record P2 (about 1 hour including setup).
2. **Owner, in parallel:** cast the narrator (audition cue-01/10/16). Book the session with the CSV cue sheet and get the licence in writing.
3. **Engineering track, separate:** Walk Lifecycle v1.1 activation on Staging. That unblocks C03/C05 (P3).
4. **Claude:** intake, then cut points, then DRAFT v1 (P5).
5. **Owner (P6):** review on iPhone and Android; sign the wordmark final visual QA; give the bed a final listen and its licence entry.
6. **Claude (P7):** final render without `--draft`, then `validate_video.py --final`.
7. Hosting and activation are separate approvals (P9).

If v1.1 activation takes long, an earlier v1 without the walk scenes is possible, but it changes the script and needs owner approval.
